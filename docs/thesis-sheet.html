Thesis structure

from first principles
Compression is intelligenceprediction and lossless compression are one object (Shannon 1948)
Part 1: Pretrainingthe claim holds
  1. Step 1Code length of a symbol is −log₂ p
  2. Step 2Expected length is H(P) + KL(P‖Q)
  3. Step 3Pretraining minimises KL to the pretraining text
  4. Step 4Smaller KL, higher benchmarks: r = −0.93 over 31 base models (Huang 2024)
Part 2: Post-trainingthe claim fails
  1. Step 5The objective moves Q toward a target, not toward general text
  2. Step 6KL to pretraining rises: +0.9 % to +16.2 % in 12 of 12 cells (exp01)
  3. Step 7The rise measures distance moved, not skill gained
  4. Step 8Distance moved causes forgetting: to test
Thesis under testFresh-text bits per byte is a 10-second forgetting meter for post-training. If Step 8 holds, a 100-checkpoint sweep costs 20 minutes of scoring instead of 200 to 300 GPU-hours of benchmarks.
Steps 1 to 4 are theorems or published measurements. Steps 5 to 7 are exp01 results. Step 8 is the open claim.
